Sign it with your own Hedera account (optional)

Your wallet signs the text of the record. The record then holds your account and its signature, and anyone can check them against your account's key on the ledger. Your account pays nothing: the site still writes the record.

A Hedera wallet, such as HashPack, connects through WalletConnect, which lists the wallets it supports. An EVM wallet, such as MetaMask, signs in this browser, for an account with an ECDSA key.
