Hedera proof
proof found: the page or its code names a Hedera ID, the ledger knows it as that kind of thing, and it was made or used at the time of the event.
found, but the dates do not fit: the ledger knows the ID, but it was not made or used at the time of the event.
found, the end of the event is not typed in yet: the ledger knows the ID, but the dates cannot be compared yet.
found, but empty: the ledger knows the ID, but nothing ever happened with it.
found, but as another kind of thing: the ledger knows the number, but as another kind than the page says.
not found: the ledger does not know this ID. cannot be checked: the ID cannot be asked about.
no Hedera ID given: neither the page nor the code names one. This says nothing bad about the project.
{% endmacro %} {% macro life_legend() %}
Life
A sign of life is a change to the code, or activity of the project's own Hedera IDs, later than 30 days after the event.
active: a sign of life in the last 90 days. quiet: a sign of life, but none in the last 90 days. stopped: nothing later than 30 days after the event.
too early to tell: the event ended less than 90 days ago. unknown: no date of the code and no activity of its own IDs.
{% endmacro %} {% macro label_legend() %}
Fields written by a model
Stated: one sentence of the page says it. Inferred: less sure: put together from several sentences, or the page only suggests it. Read it with more care.
Not found: the page says nothing about it. Removed by the quote check: none of the sentences that the model gave was found in the page, so the field is not shown.
