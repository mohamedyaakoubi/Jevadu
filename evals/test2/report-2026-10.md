# Test 2, run of October 2026: the search as it is

This is the short report of Test 2 (design 13.3). It answers one question: when a builder types an idea, is a known similar project in the first 10 results? The numbers come from `evals/results/20261014T235346Z-test2.json`. The rules are in `guideline.md`.

## What was tested

- **The search of the product:** one list by meaning, from the embeddings of the page texts, and one by words, merged. It searched all 826 projects in the database.
- **24 pairs of projects with the same idea,** each searched both ways (48 searches). 12 pairs are the same project entered again, found through a shared repository or a shared name. 12 are different projects with the same idea, found by reading the 60 pages of Test 1.
- **9 ideas written by the maintainer,** each about a project drawn at random. One repeats words of its project and is counted apart, so 8 count. One of them has two known projects.
- **15 pairs that only look alike,** for information.

## The number

| | Found in the first 10 | Range |
|---|---|---|
| All known projects | 34 of 57 (59.7%) | 44.4% to 74.1% |
| Pairs of the same idea | 29 of 48 (60.4%) | 43.8% to 77.1% |
| The maintainer's ideas | 5 of 9 (55.6%) | 22.2% to 87.5% |

**Pass mark** (design 13.6): at least 80% of the known similar projects in the first 10 results. **Not met.** As design 13.6 says, the mark is not moved, and the method screen shows this number.

## Where it works, and where it does not

- **The same project entered again** is found in 20 of 24 searches, most often in first place.
- **Different projects with the same idea** are found in 9 of 24 searches. Most misses are tools for AI agents: spending limits for agents, and markets where agents buy services. The events of 2026 hold many projects of that kind, so the first 10 places fill with other projects close to the idea. This test counts only the named project. It does not judge the other results, and some of them may be the same idea as well.
- **The maintainer's ideas:** 5 of the 8 counted ideas found their project, each time in first place. The other three were short ideas about agents, again in a crowded theme.
- **Pairs that only look alike** were shown in 2 of 30 searches. The search rarely mixes up projects that share only a name, a Hedera ID or a theme.

## Other setups, on the same cases

| Setup | Found |
|---|---|
| The product: meaning and words merged, 11 from each list | 34 of 57 |
| Meaning only | 36 of 57 |
| Words only | 23 of 57 |
| Merged, 20 from each list | 35 of 57 |
| Merged, 30 from each list | 37 of 57 |
| Merged, 50 from each list | 34 of 57 |

None finds more than the high end of the product's range (74.1%, about 42 of 57), so the product keeps its setup. The differences are of one to three searches. Words alone find much less, but merging them with meaning costs little.

## What this test does not show

- Whether the fields written by the model help the search. They exist for 75 pages only, and a fair comparison needs them for every project. That comparison is made on these same cases when the cards of all pages exist.
- Anything about another embedding model. Only one is in use.
- How often a person finds the results useful. That is Test 4, with the screens.
