# Review layer 1: S1 and S2, 15 October 2026

Layer 1 of design 12.3 for the search (S1) and the project card (S2). Tasks T1 (search an idea) and T2 (read a project card) were done on the running site, in Chromium, on a wide screen (1366 by 900) and on a phone (390 by 844). Each page was read one screen at a time from the top, as a person scrolls. The search, the filters, a card, the way back, a search that no project matches, a project that does not exist, and an idea made of letters that mean nothing were all tried.

Each finding is marked **sure** or **unsure**, and names the check of design 12.3 that it fails. Layer 2, the maintainer, accepts or rejects each one and adds new ones.

## What held

- The search gave its results in 0.8 to 2.3 seconds, and the comparison arrived 18 to 22 seconds later without moving the results.
- The three sources of the card stay apart, each with its title and colour, and each field of a model shows its label and its sentences (check 1).
- Every mark has its legend at the bottom of the page (check 6). A mark is never a colour alone.
- "Back to the search" keeps the idea and the filters (check 4).
- A search that no project matches, a project that does not exist, and a database that does not run each say what happened and what to do (check 8).
- The keyboard reaches the idea, the filters and the button, and the focus is drawn clearly.
